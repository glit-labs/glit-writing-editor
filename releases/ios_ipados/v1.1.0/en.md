# Glit for iOS Release Notes — v1.1.0

**Release date:** 2026-07-05  
**Update for iPhone · iPad**

---

## Overview

Version 1.1.0 lets you move a project between your device and the cloud — in both directions — plus a handful of refinements across sync and editing.

---

## What's New

### Upload to Cloud

Turn a local project into a cloud project. Send it to Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive and keep it in sync across your devices — no need to recreate it from scratch.

### Switch to Local Project

Turn a synced cloud project back into a local, on-device project whenever you like. Sync is unlinked and everything stays on your device.

### Refinements & Fixes

- Purchase restore now works reliably in landscape, with clearer confirmation.
- A cleaner cloud import sheet and a tidier sync menu.
- Newly created folders open automatically, so you can keep writing without an extra tap.
- Smaller refinements and fixes throughout.

---

## Requirements

- iOS / iPadOS 17.0 or later
