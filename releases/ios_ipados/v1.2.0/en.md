# Glit for iOS Release Notes — v1.2.0

**Release date:** 2026-08-07  
**Update for iPhone · iPad**

---

## Overview

Version 1.2.0 lets you add images to your Markdown documents, alongside a tidier editor options panel on iPhone.

---

## What's New

### Add Image

Place the cursor where you want the image, choose Add Image in the editing menu, then pick a photo. It is saved in an images folder next to your document and appears in preview right away.

### Images Travel With Your Project

Inserted images move with your project — they sync to Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive and open in the desktop app as well.

### Refinements & Fixes

- On iPhone, the editor options panel is grouped into clear sections, and long option names are no longer cut off.
- Smaller refinements and fixes throughout.

---

## Requirements

- iOS / iPadOS 17.0 or later
