# Glit for iOS Release Notes — v1.3.0

**Release date:** 2026-08-11  
**Update for iPhone · iPad**

---

## Overview

Version 1.3.0 brings focus writing to iPhone and iPad — a full-screen mode that leaves nothing on screen but your manuscript.

---

## What's New

### Focus Writing

Choose Start focus writing in the editor options and everything but your manuscript falls away. The line you are writing stays sharp in the center of the screen while earlier lines blur behind it.

### Character Goal & Timer

A character goal and elapsed time sit at the top. Turn on hard mode and you cannot leave until you reach the goal. Everything you write is saved straight into the episode file, as always.

### Refinements & Fixes

- On iPad, the editor options panel is now grouped into section cards, matching iPhone.
- Fixed the file name and save status being covered or cut off at the top of the editor in some languages.
- Smaller refinements and fixes throughout.

---

## Requirements

- iOS / iPadOS 17.0 or later
