# Glit for iOS Release Notes — v1.5.0

**Release date:** 2026-09-10  
**Update for iPhone · iPad**

---

## Overview

Version 1.5.0 makes sync more accurate and much faster. Deletions now travel in both directions, sync tells you exactly what happened when something fails, and large files pick up where they left off.

---

## What's New

### Deleting Syncs Both Ways

Deleting a file or folder now removes it from this device and from the cloud. Nothing disappears for good: items deleted from the cloud can be restored on the service's website, and items deleted from the device can be restored in Settings under Deleted Items.

### Overlapping Range Folders

When range folders cover overlapping episode numbers, Glit tells you and lets you fix it in the app with Clean up. Every manuscript is preserved in Version history first.

### Clearer Sync Results

Comparing with the cloud now shows the result in a popup. When sync fails, Glit tells you which files failed and why, and retries temporary errors automatically.

### Faster Comparing and Importing

Comparing and importing are much faster, and large files resume after an interruption instead of starting over.

### Refinements & Fixes

- Fixed manuscripts with Korean file names not opening in Glit for Windows.
- Smaller refinements and fixes throughout.

---

## Requirements

- iOS / iPadOS 17.0 or later
