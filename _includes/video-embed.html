{%- comment -%} Click-to-play YouTube player. Until the visitor presses play, the page shows only a self-hosted poster and makes no request to YouTube or Google — the same reason the webfonts are self-hosted (see head/custom.html). The click swaps in a youtube-nocookie.com iframe; the script for that lives on the page that includes this (ko/guide.md). Without JavaScript the link simply opens the video on YouTube. Params: id (YouTube video ID), title, poster (site path), vertical (Shorts, 9:16), eager (above-the-fold poster: load at once instead of lazily), duration (shown on the play button, e.g. "2:09"). The "전체 화면" pill starts playback straight in fullscreen. It lives on the poster only: once the player is in, nothing may sit in front of it (YouTube Required Minimum Functionality).
